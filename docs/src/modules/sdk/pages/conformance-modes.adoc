Akka Specify runs in one of two modes. The mode decides whether the xref:sdk:exit-conditions.adoc[definition of done] is a hard gate or an advisory checklist.

== The two modes

[cols="1,3"]
|===
| Mode | Behavior

| *Enforced* (default)
| A guided flow. The CLI and MCP server hard-block progress until the definition of done is complete and every condition is either met, waived, or explicitly deferred. `/akka:ship` refuses to fire until the in-scope conditions are green.

| *À la carte*
| The full set of individual commands with advisory gates. You run each phase yourself; nothing blocks you, and `/akka:ship` proceeds whether or not conditions are met.
|===

Enforced is the default because it leads with the value of spec-driven development — repeatable, auditable delivery — while auto-deriving most of the definition of done so that defining "done" up front feels guided rather than like a wall.

=== Switching modes

[source]
----
/akka:mode enforced
/akka:mode a-la-carte
----

`/akka:mode` rewrites the available command set to match the target mode and asks you to restart your assistant so the new command menu loads. A developer can switch only within the modes the organization allows; if the organization has locked the mode, the command refuses and says so. Switching out of Enforced is recorded.

== The Enforced command set

In Enforced mode the menu is deliberately small. Each command has one job:

[cols="1,3"]
|===
| Command | Role

| `/akka:specify`
| The re-entrant engine that drives the whole workflow. You always run `/akka:specify <input>` — a spec, a clarification answer, review feedback, or just "go".

| `/akka:status`
| A read-only rollup of the definition of done by business category, plus the current phase and any blockers.

| `/akka:ship`
| Fires the organization-authored terminal action, gated on every in-scope condition being green.

| `/akka:mode`
| Switches mode within the organization-allowed set.

| `/akka:setup`
| Installs and repairs the local environment and sources the governance floor.
|===

The individual phase commands from À la carte mode (clarify, plan, tasks, implement, and so on) are not separate menu entries in Enforced mode — they fold into `/akka:specify` as steps of the flow.

== The /akka:specify engine

`/akka:specify` is re-entrant: you run it repeatedly, passing whatever the moment calls for, and it always ends with a definitive status line and the exact next command to run. Every call resolves to exactly one of three states:

[cols="1,3"]
|===
| State | Meaning and next step

| *NEEDS_INPUT*
| It is your turn — a clarification, a threshold, a decision, a go-ahead, or review feedback is required. Next: `/akka:specify <your response>` (occasionally `/akka:setup`).

| *WORKING*
| It is the machine's turn — building and iterating autonomously. Next: wait, or check `/akka:status`.

| *READY_TO_SHIP*
| The completion policy is satisfied. Next: `/akka:ship`.
|===

Because every call ends in exactly one of these three states with one explicit next command, the workflow is predictable enough to follow by eye and to parse in CI.

== The Enforced flow

Enforced is two human touchpoints book-ending a silent machine phase — not a gate at every step.

. *Define* (you). Frame the feature and define done in business terms. The engine infers a full draft of the definition of done from your intent and Akka's fixed surfaces, then asks only about the residue it cannot safely infer — a threshold, a genuine fork, a type toggle. Inferred conditions are marked and do not become the contract until you confirm them. The organization's mandatory conditions are merged in, locked.
. *Build* (the machine). The engine derives exit conditions, wires auditors, plans, and implements, looping until every locally runnable auditor is green. It stops only for a short, closed list of reasons: a condition it cannot reach after repeated tries, an ambiguity that Define did not resolve, or a conflict between your definition and the organization's.
. *Review* (you). Validate the definition, not the code. A review miss becomes a new condition and loops back to Build. Close any conditions that require your sign-off. When you approve, you ship.
. *Ship*. `/akka:ship` runs the in-scope auditors and fires the organization-authored terminal action. See xref:sdk:shipping-and-receipts.adoc[Shipping and receipts].

The principle: you should not have to do your own testing until "done" is met.

== Completion is policy, not all-green

READY_TO_SHIP does not mean every condition is green. It means the *completion policy* — itself part of the definition of done, authored by the project owner — is satisfied. The policy is one of a few presets: human review after a confidence pass, a percentage-of-conditions threshold, or all-must-pass. A feature can be ready to ship with a threshold met and the remainder waived or deferred.

See xref:sdk:exit-conditions.adoc[Exit conditions and Definition of Done] for the underlying model and xref:sdk:auditors.adoc[Auditors] for how conditions are checked.
