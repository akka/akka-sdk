== Deterministic

[cols="2,3,2"]
|===
| Evaluator | Reads | Returns

| `ToolPermission`
| Tool names against an allow list, a deny list, or both. Deny outranks allow on conflict.
| `EvaluationResult` scored with the share authorised.

| `ToolCorrectness`
| Tool names against a stated expected list. Order-sensitive when configured.
| `EvaluationResult` scored with the share matching.

| `ArgumentCorrectness`
| Each tool call's parsed arguments against a stated expected map.
| `EvaluationResult` scored with the share of arguments correct.
|===

== Heuristic

[cols="2,3,2"]
|===
| Evaluator | Reads | Returns

| `LatencyBudget`
| `Interaction.latency()` against a stated `Duration`.
| `EvaluationResult` scored; half at budget, proportional above.

| `TokenBudget`
| Total input+output tokens against a limit. Attempts without measured usage are inconclusive.
| `EvaluationResult` scored, or inconclusive.

| `ModelCallBudget`
| `Interaction.modelCalls().size()` against a limit.
| `EvaluationResult` scored, or inconclusive when no calls reported.

| `RegexMatch`
| Reply against a compiled `Pattern`.
| `EvaluationResult` scored 1.0 or 0.0.

| `LengthInRange`
| Reply length against `[min, max]`, characters or words.
| `EvaluationResult` scored 1.0 or 0.0.

| `JsonValidity`
| Whether the reply parses cleanly as JSON.
| `EvaluationResult` scored 1.0 or 0.0.

| `ExactMatch`
| Reply against `evalCase.expectedOutcome()`, whitespace-normalised.
| `EvaluationResult` scored 1.0 or 0.0.

| `TokenF1`
| SQuAD-style token-overlap F1 between reply and expected outcome.
| `EvaluationResult` scored with the F1 in `[0, 1]`.

| `ContextRecall`
| `Interaction.retrievedContext()` against a customer-supplied ground-truth lookup.
| `EvaluationResult` scored with the share of relevant passages retrieved.

| `ContextPrecision`
| Retrieved passages against a customer-supplied ground-truth lookup.
| `EvaluationResult` scored with the share of retrieved passages that were relevant.

| `ReciprocalRank`
| `Interaction.retrievedContext()` ordered; ground-truth lookup for the relevance.
| `EvaluationResult` scored with 1 / rank of the first relevant passage.

| `NormalizedDiscountedCumulativeGain`
| Retrieved passages ordered against a ground-truth lookup.
| `EvaluationResult` scored with NDCG@K in `[0, 1]`.
|===

== Agentic

Every agentic evaluator extends `AlignmentMetric`, takes an `Assessor` in the constructor, and ships a `systemPrompt()` in its class.

[cols="2,3,2"]
|===
| Evaluator | The model judges | Returns

| `TaskCompletion`
| Whether the reply carried out the task.
| `EvaluationResult` scored from the assessor's score.

| `PlanQuality`
| Whether the plan the agent formed would achieve the task.
| `EvaluationResult` scored from the assessor's score.

| `PlanAdherence`
| Whether the agent followed the plan it formed.
| `EvaluationResult` scored from the assessor's score.

| `StepEfficiency`
| Whether the step sequence was efficient for the task.
| `EvaluationResult` scored from the assessor's score.

| `TurnFaithfulness`
| Whether the reply is supported by the retrieved passages.
| `EvaluationResult` scored from the assessor's score.

| `TurnRelevancy`
| Whether the reply addresses the question that was asked.
| `EvaluationResult` scored from the assessor's score.

| `CitationFaithfulness`
| Whether each citation marker points at a supporting passage.
| `EvaluationResult` scored from the assessor's score.
|===
