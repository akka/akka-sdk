= Data flows
:page-aliases: operations:technical-overview/data-flows-grid.adoc
:page-layout: dense
:page-breadcrumb: Akka / Deployment / How AAO works / Data flows
:page-summary: Every network flow between your environment and the Akka Automated Operations platform in one filterable grid, for security review.

[.dense, cols="2,5,2,2,4,4", options="header"]
|===
| Actor | Flow | From | To | Connectivity | Auth

6+h| Your end users

| End users | End user accessing an Akka service from outside your environment | End user | Akka Region | [.conn-public]#Public# (through your firewall) | Your keys, secrets, and tokens
| End users | End user accessing an Akka service from inside your environment | End user | Akka Region | [.conn-private]#Private# (peering) | Your keys, secrets, and tokens

6+h| Your deployed Akka services

| Deployed services | Access to your private internal systems | Your service | Your APIs | [.conn-private]#Private# (peering) | Your keys, secrets, and tokens
| Deployed services | Pushing observability metrics for your services to your observability | Akka Region | Your observability | [.conn-private]#Private# (peering) | Your keys, secrets, and tokens

6+h| Your operators, admins, and developers

| Operators and developers | Akka CLI or console authentication initiation | CLI or console | Federation Plane | [.conn-public]#Public# (firewall, Federation Plane allowlisted) | Akka authentication
| Operators and developers | OpenID authentication | Console | Your IAM | [.conn-public]#Public# | OpenID
| Operators and developers | Add a region to a project | CLI | Federation Plane | [.conn-public]#Public# (firewall, Federation Plane allowlisted) | Akka authenticated, valid access token
| Operators and developers | Initiate multi-region failover | CLI | Akka Region | [.conn-private]#Private# (peering) | Akka authenticated, valid access token
| Operators and developers | List services and projects | CLI | Akka Region | [.conn-private]#Private# (peering) | Akka authenticated, valid access token
| Operators and developers | Create a project | CLI | Federation Plane | [.conn-public]#Public# (firewall, Federation Plane allowlisted) | Akka authenticated, valid access token
| Operators and developers | Add a user, role, or permission | CLI | Federation Plane | [.conn-public]#Public# (firewall, Federation Plane allowlisted) | Akka authenticated, valid access token
| Operators and developers | Inspect service components in a region | CLI | Akka Region | [.conn-private]#Private# (peering) | Akka authenticated, valid access token
| Operators and developers | Deploy a service: initiate deployment | CI/CD or developer | Akka Region | [.conn-private]#Private# (peering) | Akka authenticated, valid access token
| Operators and developers | Deploy a service: region pulls the image | Akka Region | Your image registry | [.conn-private]#Private# (peering) | Akka authenticated, valid access token

6+h| Akka SRE

| Akka SRE | Troubleshoot a region (cloud console or kubectl) | Your VDI, when needed | Akka Region | [.conn-private]#Private# (your remote desktop) | Your cloud credentials, granted through Teleport

6+h| Akka Region

| Akka Region | Region observability metadata to the Federation Plane | Akka Region | Federation Plane | [.conn-public]#Public# (Akka NAT gateway) | Kubernetes RBAC and Federation Plane observability token
| Akka Region | Region requests the public key set | Akka Region | Federation Plane | [.conn-public]#Public# (Akka NAT gateway) | Internal Akka JWT authentication
| Akka Region | Certificate expiration monitoring (can be disabled) | Akka Region | Akka SRE | [.conn-public]#Public# (Akka NAT gateway) | Kubernetes RBAC
| Akka Region | Replication to another Akka Region | Akka Region | Akka Region | [.conn-private]#Private# (peering) | Mutual TLS and ACLs

6+h| Akka Federation Plane (all flows use the bootstrap credential you create and share with Akka)

| Federation Plane | Bootstrap a new Akka Region | Federation Plane | Akka Region | [.conn-public]#Public# (pre-authorized IP ranges) |
| Federation Plane | Install a new version of Akka into a region | Federation Plane | Akka Region | [.conn-public]#Public# (pre-authorized IP ranges) |
| Federation Plane | Maintain infrastructure (drift detection) | Federation Plane | Akka Region | [.conn-public]#Public# (pre-authorized IP ranges) |
| Federation Plane | Upgrade infrastructure (Kubernetes, database) | Federation Plane | Akka Region | [.conn-public]#Public# (pre-authorized IP ranges) |
| Federation Plane | Multi-region root CA management for cross-region mTLS | Federation Plane | Akka Region | [.conn-public]#Public# (pre-authorized IP ranges) |
| Federation Plane | Synchronize project, user, role, organization, and region metadata | Federation Plane | Akka Region | [.conn-public]#Public# (pre-authorized IP ranges) |
|===
