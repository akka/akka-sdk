= Cost, training, and evaluation commands
:page-summary: The akka optimize commands for spend, incidents, pricing, model training, dataset curation, and evaluation.
:page-persona: builder-ai-ml, operator
:page-banner: inference

include::ROOT:partial$include.adoc[]

These commands cover what an organization spends on inference and how models are trained and scored. Every command on this page is a subcommand of `akka optimize`. This page lists the commands most tasks use. See xref:reference:cli/akka-cli/akka_optimize.adoc[akka optimize] for every command and flag.

The format a `-f FILE` option reads depends on the command, and `--help` states it. Training plans are JSON or YAML. `eval datasets create` reads a zip or an `items.jsonl` file. `datasets create` reads chat-format JSONL. `training sessions create` reads a conversation corpus. Most commands read the document from standard input when you pass `-f -`.

== Spend

`akka optimize spend` reports what a period consumed, what it cost, and what one conversation cost. The alias `spends` is accepted.

[cols="2,3", options="header"]
|===
| Command
| Description

| `akka optimize spend get --from INSTANT --to INSTANT`
| What a period consumed and cost, divided as asked.

| `akka optimize spend compare --from INSTANT --to INSTANT --against-from INSTANT --against-to INSTANT`
| Two periods, and the difference between them.

| `akka optimize spend conversations list --from INSTANT --to INSTANT`
| The conversations of a period that cost the most.

| `akka optimize spend info`
| Which service is answering at this address.
|===

== Callers

`akka optimize callers` holds the callers that spend figures are attributed to. The alias `caller` is accepted.

[cols="2,3", options="header"]
|===
| Command
| Description

| `akka optimize callers erase VALUE --basis BASIS`
| Remove one caller and keep every figure it is behind. `BASIS` is `declared` when the request carried the `x-optimize-caller` header, or `forwarded` when a proxy reported the address.

| `akka optimize callers erase VALUE --basis BASIS --dry-run`
| Report what the erasure would reach without writing anything.
|===

== Incidents

`akka optimize incidents` is the incident feed, unioned from its sources. The alias `incident` is accepted.

[cols="2,3", options="header"]
|===
| Command
| Description

| `akka optimize incidents list`
| The incident feed: anomalies, blocked requests and scrub failures.
|===

== Pricing catalog

`akka optimize pricing-catalog` holds model prices as input and output rates per million tokens for each served name. A published version is never edited. The aliases `pricing-catalogs`, `catalog`, and `catalogs` are accepted.

[cols="2,3", options="header"]
|===
| Command
| Description

| `akka optimize pricing-catalog get`
| The version in force now, or at an instant.

| `akka optimize pricing-catalog apply -f FILE`
| Publish a catalog version, or check one.

| `akka optimize pricing-catalog versions list`
| Every published version, newest first.
|===

== Training runs

`akka optimize training runs` manages training runs. The alias `run` is accepted.

[cols="2,3", options="header"]
|===
| Command
| Description

| `akka optimize training runs start -f PLAN`
| Start a training run from a plan file.

| `akka optimize training runs list`
| List training runs.

| `akka optimize training runs get RUN`
| Show one run.

| `akka optimize training runs wait RUN`
| Wait for a run to finish.

| `akka optimize training runs cancel RUN`
| Cancel a run and free its GPUs.
|===

== Training pipelines

`akka optimize training pipelines` manages multi-stage training pipelines. The alias `pipeline` is accepted.

[cols="2,3", options="header"]
|===
| Command
| Description

| `akka optimize training pipelines start -f PLAN`
| Start a pipeline from a plan file.

| `akka optimize training pipelines list`
| List pipelines and the stage each is on.

| `akka optimize training pipelines get PIPELINE`
| Show a pipeline, stage by stage.

| `akka optimize training pipelines wait PIPELINE`
| Wait for every stage to finish.

| `akka optimize training pipelines cancel PIPELINE`
| Cancel a pipeline and its running stage.
|===

== Trained models

`akka optimize trained-models` manages the models training produced. The alias `trained-model` is accepted.

[cols="2,3", options="header"]
|===
| Command
| Description

| `akka optimize trained-models list`
| List models and what they scored.

| `akka optimize trained-models get MODEL`
| Show one model's lineage, cost, and score.
|===

== Training evaluations

`akka optimize training evaluations` manages evaluations of trained models. The alias `evaluation` is accepted.

[cols="2,3", options="header"]
|===
| Command
| Description

| `akka optimize training evaluations start --model MODEL --dataset HASH`
| Score a trained model against a dataset.

| `akka optimize training evaluations list`
| List evaluations and what they scored.

| `akka optimize training evaluations get EVALUATION`
| Show what an evaluation scored and where it got to.

| `akka optimize training evaluations report EVALUATION`
| Show how a model scored, field by field.

| `akka optimize training evaluations predictions EVALUATION`
| Show what the model answered, example by example.

| `akka optimize training evaluations wait EVALUATION`
| Follow an evaluation until it finishes.

| `akka optimize training evaluations cancel EVALUATION`
| Stop an evaluation and release what it holds.
|===

== Training datasets

`akka optimize datasets` manages training datasets. The alias `dataset` is accepted.

[cols="2,3", options="header"]
|===
| Command
| Description

| `akka optimize datasets create -f FILE`
| Register chat-format JSONL examples. The alias `register` is accepted.

| `akka optimize datasets list`
| List registered datasets, newest first.

| `akka optimize datasets get HASH`
| Show what a dataset holds.

| `akka optimize datasets name NAME DATASET`
| Point a name at a registered dataset.

| `akka optimize datasets images push PATH...`
| Upload images, skipping the ones already registered.
|===

== Sessions and curations

A session is a raw conversation corpus. A curation judges raw sessions into a training dataset.

[cols="2,3", options="header"]
|===
| Command
| Description

| `akka optimize training sessions create -f CORPUS --source SOURCE`
| Upload a raw conversation corpus.

| `akka optimize training curations apply -f FILE`
| Start a curation run.

| `akka optimize training curations wait CURATION`
| Wait for a curation to finish.

| `akka optimize training curations get ID`
| Phase, counts, mean score and resulting dataset.
|===

== Evaluation

`akka optimize eval` covers datasets, experiments and the evaluators that score them.

[cols="2,3", options="header"]
|===
| Command
| Description

| `akka optimize eval datasets create -f FILE`
| Upload a dataset file and version it.

| `akka optimize eval datasets list`
| Datasets that have been imported, most recently imported first.

| `akka optimize eval datasets get ID`
| One dataset: what it holds, what scores it, and every version it has had.

| `akka optimize eval datasets items ID --version V`
| The items in one version of a dataset.

| `akka optimize eval datasets imports get ID`
| How far an import has got.

| `akka optimize eval experiments apply -f FILE`
| Start an experiment.

| `akka optimize eval experiments get ID`
| How far an experiment has got, and what it has found.

| `akka optimize eval experiments record ID`
| The finished record: the run's report, or the numbers behind it.

| `akka optimize eval experiments item EXPERIMENT ITEM`
| One item's run, and what each evaluator said about it.

| `akka optimize eval experiments compare ID --baseline ID`
| An experiment against a baseline, item by item.

| `akka optimize eval experiments history NAME`
| Every run of a recurring experiment, most recent first.

| `akka optimize eval evaluators list`
| Registered evaluators, their kind and their version.
|===

== Captures

`akka optimize captures` reads recorded interaction artifacts off a filesystem. The alias `capture` is accepted.

These commands serve debugging and diagnostics. They read artifacts from a directory on disk, which is where a capture written during local development or a support investigation lands.

[NOTE]
====
Captures recorded in production are stored in a location the CLI cannot reach in this version. The commands below read a filesystem directory only.
====

[cols="2,3", options="header"]
|===
| Command
| Description

| `akka optimize captures list DIRECTORY`
| Every artifact in a directory, newest first.

| `akka optimize captures show ARTIFACT`
| One artifact, read forward.
|===
