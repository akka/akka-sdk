= Choose how you run Akka
:page-aliases: operations:akka-platform.adoc
:page-summary: Akka runs in five deployment options: self-managed on infrastructure you choose, or operated by Akka Automated Operations on Serverless, Dedicated, BYOC or BYOK8s. This page defines each option and compares them.
:page-when-to-use: When you are choosing where your Akka services run and who operates the platform under them.
:page-related: operations:technical-overview.adoc, operations:production-readiness/choosing-your-environment.adoc, operations:production-readiness/index.adoc

You run Akka in one of five deployment options. In the self-managed option you operate Akka yourself. In the other four, Akka Automated Operations (AAO) operates the platform for you: it handles deployment, scaling, multi-region replication, patching and recovery, so you deploy a service and AAO runs it. Your services are the same code in every option.

== The five options

[cols="1,2,2,1", options="header"]
|===
| Option | Who runs the platform | Whose cloud account, and who pays for it | Tenancy

| Self-managed
| You
| Infrastructure you choose: bare metal, VMs, containers, PaaS or edge. You pay its provider.
| Yours

| Serverless
| Akka, with AAO
| Akka's cloud account. You pay Akka as you go, by credit card.
| Multi-tenant

| Dedicated
| Akka, with AAO
| Akka's cloud account. Akka bills you for the region.
| Single-tenant

| BYOC
| Akka, with AAO
| Your cloud account on AWS, Azure or GCP. You pay your cloud provider for the infrastructure.
| Single-tenant

| BYOK8s
| Akka operates the platform, you own the cluster
| A Kubernetes cluster you provide, in your cloud account. You pay your cloud provider.
| Single-tenant
|===

Dedicated and BYOC are one delivery model in two places: Dedicated is a single-tenant region in Akka's account, and BYOC is the same region in your account. BYOK8s is BYOC where you provide the Kubernetes cluster. Dedicated adds a private data link to your network; BYOC does not need one, because the region already runs in your account.

In BYOC and BYOK8s, application data stays in your environment. A limited set of platform interactions, such as deployments and key rotation, still crosses to the Akka Federation Plane. xref:operations:technical-overview.adoc[How AAO works] describes the architecture, and xref:operations:technical-overview/data-flows.adoc[] lists every flow.

== What AAO adds

AAO is built on a Kubernetes-based control plane and application plane designed to run Akka services. It manages, monitors and gathers insights from your deployed services:

* Deployments, scaling and recovery with no operator steps.
* Multi-region replication with failover for business-critical workloads.
* Logs, metrics and traces from the first deployment.
* Dedicated infrastructure for regulatory or data-sovereignty requirements, in Dedicated, BYOC and BYOK8s.

AAO monitors region health and brings replacement capacity online without operator action.

== Availability

Every option that AAO operates runs each service across availability zones in a region, so the loss of one data center recovers automatically. A project can also span several regions, with state replicated between them. Replication between regions is asynchronous: when a region is lost, writes that it accepted and had not yet replicated to another region are not available in the other regions. Moving traffic away from a failed region is an operator action with the Akka CLI. Recovery targets are set in your agreement with Akka, and the link:{url-akka-availability-guarantee}[Availability Guarantee Policy, window="new"] states the conditions of the Akka availability guarantee, which requires multi-region. See xref:operations:production-readiness/choosing-your-environment.adoc[] to choose between zones and multi-region.
