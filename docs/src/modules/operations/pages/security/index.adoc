Akka controls access to organizations and projects with role-based access control (RBAC). A _permission_ allows one operation, such as `project.services.get`. A _role_ is a named group of permissions. A _role binding_ grants a role to a _principal_.

A principal is one of the following:

* A user.
* A group of users from an xref:operations:security/oidc-setup.adoc[OpenID Connect] provider.
* An Akka service, using xref:operations:services/workload-identity.adoc[workload identity].
* A workload identity from an external identity provider.

The xref:reference:rbac/permissions.adoc[Permissions] reference lists every permission. The xref:reference:rbac/roles.adoc[Roles] reference lists the built-in roles and the permissions they grant.

== Built-in roles

Akka provides built-in roles at two levels.

Organization roles::
`superuser`, `project-admin`, `billing-admin` and `member`. They grant permissions on the organization, for example to manage its role bindings, create projects or view billing.

Project roles::
`admin`, `developer`, `viewer` and `backoffice`. They grant permissions on the services, routes, secrets and other resources of one project.

The `member` organization role allows project admins to add the user to their projects without sending an invitation to the organization.

The xref:reference:rbac/roles.adoc[Roles] reference shows the project permissions that each organization role includes. A superuser can assign roles in the projects of the organization, including to themselves. When a user creates a project, Akka grants the user the `admin` role on that project.

== Role bindings at the organization and project levels

You create role bindings at two levels:

* An xref:operations:security/organization-role-bindings.adoc[organization role binding] grants an organization role, or a custom role, in the whole organization.
* A xref:operations:security/project-role-bindings.adoc[project role binding] grants a project role in one project.

A project role binding only grants access to its project. Organization membership does not grant access to any project.

== Custom roles

The built-in roles may grant more or less than you want. A xref:operations:security/custom-roles.adoc[custom role] defines your own group of permissions.

Custom roles are always defined in an organization, and you bind them with organization role bindings. You can not bind a custom role at the project level. A custom role limits where its permissions apply by naming the projects in its rules. For example, one custom role can grant deployment permissions on two projects and read-only access to all the others.

The following table helps you choose.

[cols="2,3,3"]
|===
| Need | Use | Where to bind

| Give a user a standard level of access to one project
| A built-in project role
| xref:operations:security/project-role-bindings.adoc[Project role binding]

| Give a user a standard level of access to the organization
| A built-in organization role
| xref:operations:security/organization-role-bindings.adoc[Organization role binding]

| Give a user specific permissions, or different access to different projects
| A xref:operations:security/custom-roles.adoc[custom role]
| xref:operations:security/organization-role-bindings.adoc[Organization role binding]
|===

== Single sign-on

Akka supports single sign-on through OpenID Connect. With OpenID Connect, you can bind roles to groups from your identity provider instead of to individual users. See xref:operations:security/oidc-setup.adoc[].
