= RACI (BYOK8s)
:page-summary: The complete BYOK8s (Bring Your Own Kubernetes) RACI matrix, listing every operational activity with its Responsible, Accountable, Consulted, and Informed split, plus descriptions and cadences.
:page-persona: operator-platform, operator-sre

include::ROOT:partial$include.adoc[]

This page lists every activity in the BYOK8s (Bring Your Own Kubernetes) shared responsibility model, where Akka runs inside a Kubernetes cluster that the Customer provides and operates. Each activity is split across the four RACI roles:

* *R (Responsible):* the party that does the work.
* *A (Accountable):* the party ultimately answerable for the outcome.
* *C (Consulted):* the party whose input is sought before or during the work.
* *I (Informed):* the party kept up to date on progress or results.

In BYOK8s the Customer owns more of the stack than in a fully Akka-managed model. For example, database backups and Kubernetes/database upgrades are Customer-owned here.

Each RACI cell uses one of the following:

[cols="1,4", options="header"]
|===
| Cell | Meaning

| C
| Customer

| A
| Akka

| ±
| Akka and Customer

| (blank)
| Not applicable
|===

Anything not explicitly listed in this matrix is the Customer's responsibility.
