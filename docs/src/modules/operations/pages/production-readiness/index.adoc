== Overview

Production readiness is a partnership with Akka, not something you do alone. Akka provides the platform and years of operating distributed systems in production at scale; your team owns a clearly defined set of activities. How much of the running and the reliability each side owns is set out in a Shared Responsibility Model, and shifts with the xref:concepts:deployment-model.adoc[deployment model] you choose.

Akka's team embeds with yours from onboarding through ongoing operations. The readiness disciplines this page outlines are what that production experience has shown to be necessary, worked through with you rather than discovered under pressure. Contact your Akka team to walk through the Shared Responsibility Model and plan a production-readiness review.

== Production readiness

The disciplines that decide whether go-live is smooth. Each links to the relevant operations guidance.

Sizing and capacity::
Forecast load and agree compute and persistence shapes before launch. See xref:operations:regions/index.adoc[] and xref:operations:regions/infrastructure.adoc[].

Observability::
Export application logs, metrics, and traces to your own tooling, and confirm database and persistence health is monitored. See xref:operations:observability-and-monitoring/index.adoc[].

Delivery and rollback::
Automate deployment and rehearse rollback, not only of container images but of configuration and data. See xref:operations:integrating-cicd/index.adoc[] and xref:operations:services/deploy-service.adoc[].

Backups and recovery::
Confirm database and Kubernetes-state backups are configured, monitored, and retained to meet your recovery point objective, and that a restore has been tested. See xref:operations:services/data-management.adoc[] and xref:operations:operator-best-practices.adoc[].

Multi-region resilience::
Choose replication behaviour per service and rehearse failover and failback. See xref:operations:regions/index.adoc#selecting-primary[Selecting primary for stateful components] and xref:operations:operator-best-practices.adoc[].

Certificates::
Akka rotates platform-managed TLS ahead of expiry. Monitor and rehearse rotation for any customer-managed certificates. See xref:operations:tls-certificates.adoc[].

Ownership::
Name a single individual accountable for production readiness end-to-end, with the authority to declare readiness or hold the launch.

== How the split shifts by deployment model

Akka behaves identically across xref:concepts:deployment-model.adoc[deployment models]. What changes is how much of the operational surface you run:

* *Dedicated*: Akka hosts and operates the platform and connects to you over a private data link. Your team carries the least.
* *BYOC (Bring Your Own Cloud)*: Akka operates the platform inside your own cloud account. You own the account, networking, and data boundary.
* *BYOK8s (Bring Your Own Kubernetes)*: you run Akka on the Kubernetes cluster you operate and own more of the stack, including the cluster, its upgrades, and backups. See xref:operations:configuring.adoc[Self-managed operations].

The further you move toward BYOK8s, the more of the readiness items fall to your team.
