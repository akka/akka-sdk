Serverless runs your Akka services on Akka's shared cloud. Akka Automated Operations (AAO) operates the platform around the clock, so there is no infrastructure for you to set up or run.

== How Serverless works

You pay as you go for what your services use, by credit card.

Capacity follows load in both directions. A service that receives no traffic scales to zero, and scales back up when requests arrive.

Akka operates the platform 24/7, including patches, certificate and key rotation, and updates with no downtime.

Serverless regions run on every major hyperscaler, in several regions across different territories. See xref:operations:organizations/regions.adoc[] for the regions your organization can use and how to request more.

You deploy straight from the Akka CLI.

Serverless is multi-tenant: your services share infrastructure with other organizations. For a single-tenant region, see xref:operations:technical-overview/dedicated.adoc[Dedicated] or xref:operations:technical-overview/byoc.adoc[BYOC].
