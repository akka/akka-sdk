== Overview

Akka runs the platform and owns its reliability. A defined set of activities stays with your team, and the split shifts with your xref:concepts:deployment-model.adoc[deployment model]. This page is a high-level map of that division and of what "production-ready" means on your side. It complements the per-area guidance across xref:operations:index.adoc[Operating].

[NOTE]
====
This page is a general reference and evolves over time. The authoritative, tailored readiness plan for your account is produced and maintained with your Akka team.
====

== How responsibility is divided

[cols="1,3", options="header"]
|===
|Owner |Responsibilities

|*Akka*
|The platform and runtime, elastic scaling, patching, multi-region replication, self-healing recovery, platform observability, and 24/7 SRE. See xref:operations:akka-platform.adoc[Akka Automated Operations].

|*Shared*
|Sizing and capacity planning, disaster-recovery exercises, onboarding and go-live, and backups (the exact split depends on the deployment model).

|*You*
|Your application and its data, service configuration and secrets, application-level access control, application observability, and the go-live readiness items below.
|===

Anything not explicitly owned by Akka is the customer's responsibility.

== How the split shifts by deployment model

Akka behaves identically across xref:concepts:deployment-model.adoc[deployment models]. What changes is how much of the operational surface you run:

* *Akka Automated Operations (serverless)*: Akka hosts and operates everything. Your team carries the least.
* *Akka Automated Operations (in your VPC)*: Akka operates the platform inside your own cloud account. You own the account, networking, and data boundary.
* *Self-managed operations*: you run Akka on your own infrastructure and own more of the stack, including the cluster, its upgrades, and backups. See xref:operations:configuring.adoc[Self-managed operations].

The further you move toward self-managed, the more of the readiness items below fall to your team.

== Production readiness

The disciplines that decide whether go-live is smooth. Each links to the relevant operations guidance.

Sizing and capacity::
Forecast load and agree compute and persistence shapes before launch. See xref:operations:regions/index.adoc[] and xref:operations:regions/infrastructure.adoc[].

Observability::
Export application logs, metrics, and traces to your own tooling, and confirm database and persistence health is monitored. See xref:operations:observability-and-monitoring/index.adoc[].

Delivery and rollback::
Automate deployment and rehearse rollback, not only of container images but of configuration and data. See xref:operations:integrating-cicd/index.adoc[] and xref:operations:services/deploy-service.adoc[].

Backups and recovery::
Confirm database and Kubernetes-state backups are configured, monitored, and retained to meet your recovery point objective, and that a restore has been tested. See xref:operations:services/data-management.adoc[] and xref:operations:operator-best-practices.adoc[].

Multi-region resilience::
Choose replication behaviour per service and rehearse failover and failback. See xref:operations:regions/index.adoc#selecting-primary[Selecting primary for stateful components] and xref:operations:operator-best-practices.adoc[].

Certificates::
Akka rotates platform-managed TLS ahead of expiry. Monitor and rehearse rotation for any customer-managed certificates. See xref:operations:tls-certificates.adoc[].

Ownership::
Name a single individual accountable for production readiness end-to-end, with the authority to declare readiness or hold the launch.

== Getting to production with Akka

Your Akka team walks these items with you ahead of go-live and stays engaged through operations. Contact your Akka team to plan a production-readiness review.
